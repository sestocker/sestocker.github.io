I've given this talk in Chicago, Cleveland and LA Sitecore User Groups. I've had great conversations in all those settings. There is high interest for headless as an architecture as Sitecore development is moving in that direction.

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide2.PNG" width="500" alt="about me" />  
Hi, I'm Scott. I'm a Senior Solutions Architect at [Perficient](https://www.perficient.com/). I've been working with Sitecore since version 6.2. It's been a _long_ time I've been in the Sitecore ecosystem.

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide4.PNG" width="500" alt="Sitecore evolution" />
Sitecore development has evolved a ton over the years. 

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide5.PNG" width="500" alt="Sitecore evolution timeline" />  
I started way back in the WebForms days. Then of course we went to MVC and much cleaner architecture. The next big evolution was SXA - which evolved MVC, had an opinioned way to do development and introduced variants as rendering templates stored in Sitecore. Headless with the layout service and initial version of JSS came not too long after. Finally, XM Cloud was introduced in 2022 and headless development started to get some real momentum. All of these have substantially changed the way that Sitecore development has been done. The latest evolution towards headless is an entirely different technology stack!

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide6.PNG" width="500" alt="lets talk about headless" />  
I think most of the Sitecore community knows headless today. I mean basically “Headless” and XM Cloud are synonymous.

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide7.PNG" width="500" alt="xm cloud" />  
The "what" for headless is XM Cloud. You can do headless on self hosted XP or XM but the adoption of headless as an architecture in the Sitecore world took off with XM Cloud. Services like Vercel or Netlify to host the frontend although there are a lot of options here as well.

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide8.PNG" width="500" alt="JSS" />  
The “how” is the toolsets that if you are a developer you know – React/Next.js, ASP.NET SDK, etc.
Basically the “how” are the Sitecore provided SDK’s and the development frameworks your team uses to build the headless solution.

<img src="/images/talks/whats-the-point-of-headless/Slide9.PNG" width="500" alt="why" />  
But WHY?
Especially for Sitecore devs used to .NET and backend technologies, the transition is a huge one.
Going from SXA / MVC to “headless” is a complete re-write.
